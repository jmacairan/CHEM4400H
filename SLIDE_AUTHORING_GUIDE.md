# CHEM 4400H slide-authoring standard

## Structure-first examples

Every named chemical example must include its structure on the same slide. Mechanism and fragmentation slides should normally present a visual sequence:

**parent structure → charged product or adduct + neutral fragment**

The visual must identify the species that produces the discussed peak. Charges, radical dots, isotopic labels, protonation sites, and adducts should be visible rather than left implicit in prose.

For proteins and other macromolecules, use an authoritative ribbon, surface, or atomic structure instead of attempting an unreadable small-molecule-style drawing. When the full identity is not specified, show only a clearly labelled representative scaffold.

Structure graphics should support the teaching point without replacing the existing explanation, spectrum, calculation, or interaction.

All structure graphics must use the shared click-to-enlarge viewer. They should be keyboard focusable, open at a legible high resolution with Enter or Space, and close with Escape or the visible close button.

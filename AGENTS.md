# CHEM 4400H slide requirements

These rules apply to every new or revised lecture slide in this folder.

## Chemical structures

- Whenever a slide names or discusses a specific molecule, show an accurate chemical-structure graphic on that same slide.
- When fragmentation, rearrangement, ionization, adduct formation, or neutral loss is discussed, show the parent structure and the chemically relevant product ion or neutral fragment whenever that visual helps explain the pathway.
- Mark charge, radical character, isotopes, protonation or metal-adduct sites, and retained connectivity explicitly. Use proper HTML subscripts and superscripts in labels.
- Do not use a decorative generic molecule in place of the compound being discussed. If the exact derivative is unspecified, label any displayed core as representative and state that it is not the full compound.
- Prefer standardized 2D depictions from PubChem for small molecules and an authoritative structural database such as RCSB PDB for biomolecules. Record the source in the image metadata, alt text, caption, or speaker notes.
- Keep structures large enough to read in presentation mode and give every structure meaningful alternative text.
- Every structure graphic must support click-to-enlarge and keyboard enlargement so students can inspect it at high resolution. The enlarged view must close with Escape and expose an accessible close control.
- Preserve the existing lecture text, interactions, spectra, and layout unless a small adjustment is required to fit the structure or correct chemistry exposed by the visual.
